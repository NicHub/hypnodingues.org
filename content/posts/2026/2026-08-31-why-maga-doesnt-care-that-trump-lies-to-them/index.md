## Transcript

Why Trump supporters don’t care that Trump is lying to them. There’s a psychological reason for that, and it’s more disturbing than you think.

Political scientist David Moon at the University of Bath published peer-reviewed research on exactly this. He called it _kayfabe_.[^1]

In professional wrestling, everyone knows the fights are fake. The outcomes are scripted. The rivalries are performed. And the fans don’t care, because they’re not there for reality. They’re there for the villain, the drama, the spectacle. They’re there for the show.[^2]

Moon found that Trump supporters engage with politics the exact same way as pro wrestling. Trump promised no new wars — we are in a war. He promised cheap gas prices — gas prices are over $4. He promised lower bills — they’ve all gone up. And they don’t care, because they were never voting for results. They were voting for the show. They were voting for their favorite pro wrestler.[^3]

Now, here’s where the psychology gets a little dark. In wrestling, the villain is called the _heel_, and the heel is always two things at once — unstoppable and a victim at the same time. Sound familiar? Trump perfected the role of the heel. He is the most powerful man in the world and yet claims to be the most persecuted victim in the world. His supporters hold both of these ideas at the same time, without any conflict, because pro wrestling trained them to do exactly that — to turn off their sense of reality and enjoy the show, no matter who it hurts.[^4]

This is why facts don’t work on Trump supporters. You’re trying to have a policy debate with someone watching professional wrestling. These people would burn their own house down and, an hour later, sit in the rubble of their burned-down house and watch it burning down on TV for entertainment. Their jobs are here. Their kids go to school here. Their parents collect Social Security here. And they applaud the man torching it all, because the show is too good to turn off.[^5]

Our country is not professional wrestling, and anyone willing to burn it down for entertainment cannot be trusted with power — ever again. Because the next time, there may not be anything left to save. Our country is not a wrestling ring. And when the show is finally over, the damage is real. The people who did this knew exactly what they were watching. They just didn’t care. That should be unforgivable. And the least we should do is never allow them anywhere near power, ever again.
